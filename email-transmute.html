Email Transmute Available Now

An Outlook add-in that saves emails and attachments as PDF — directly to SharePoint, OneDrive, or your device. One click from the reading pane, or bulk archive an entire mail folder in one go.

✓ 100% M365 native — no data leaves your tenant

Merge or keep originals

When an email has attachments, choose how they are saved:

  • Single PDF — email body and all supported attachments are converted and merged into one searchable PDF
  • Subfolder — email is saved as a PDF; original files (Word, Excel, images, etc.) are saved alongside it in a named subfolder

Emails without attachments always save as a single PDF regardless of mode.

Bulk archive an entire mail folder

Archive a whole folder at once

Select any Outlook mail folder and archive every email in it — no manual processing one by one.

  • Browse your full Outlook folder tree with email counts
  • Deduplicate by conversation — keep only the latest email per thread
  • Choose merge or separate mode for attachments
  • Save to SharePoint, or download the whole archive as a ZIP
  • Failed emails are skipped and logged — the archive continues regardless
  • Progress shown per email during processing
