About Arcane Technologies

Rooted in Cumbria, Arcane Technologies is the IT consultancy that doesn't hide behind jargon, helpdesk tickets, or a suit. We tell you what's wrong, what it'll cost, and what we'd actually recommend — even if that means telling you to spend less.

We've worked across the education sector, local government, civil service, defence, and the third sector, in the UK, Germany, Africa, and the Middle East.

We've seen creaking infrastructure, decade-old servers holding entire organisations hostage, software that predates smartphones still somehow running payroll; and more cursed printers than any human being should have to deal with.

The fix is always the same: honest advice, plain English, and actually getting it done. No call centres. No scripts. No holding music. Just experienced IT support and a bit of Northern common sense.
